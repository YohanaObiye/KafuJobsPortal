KAFU Jobs - Help & Support

Welcome to the KAFU Jobs app! Below are some common questions and answers to help you navigate and use the app effectively.

📌 How to Apply for a Job?

1. Browse available job listings in the **Jobs** section.

2. Click on a job to view details.

3. Click **Apply Now** and upload the required documents.

4. Submit your application.

📌 How to Check Application Status?

1. Go to the **My Applications** section.

2. Your submitted applications will be listed.

3. The **status** (Pending, Approved, or Rejected) will be displayed next to each application.

📌 How Will I Receive Notifications?

1. Notifications will be sent to your app’s **Notifications** tab.

2. Important updates will also appear in your phone’s **notification bar**.

3. Ensure you have enabled notifications in your device settings.
